# AtliQ Hardware | Business Insights 360

##  Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI for AtliQ Hardware, a consumer electronics company operating across multiple markets and sales channels.

The project transforms business data into interactive dashboards that help different departments monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

The solution covers five major business functions:

* Finance
* Sales
* Marketing
* Supply Chain
* Executive Management

---

##  Business Context

AtliQ Hardware sells consumer electronic products through multiple sales channels:

* **Direct Channel:** AtliQ's own digital platform and physical brick-and-mortar stores.
* **Retailer Channel:** Sales through third-party retailers.
* **Distributor Channel:** Sales through distribution partners in selected markets.

With data spread across different business functions, decision-makers need a centralized reporting solution to monitor performance across the organization.

This project brings important business metrics and insights together through a unified Power BI reporting solution.

---

##  Project Objectives

* Build a centralized Business Intelligence solution.
* Analyze financial performance and Profit & Loss trends.
* Track sales performance across customers, products and markets.
* Evaluate marketing and market performance.
* Monitor supply chain performance.
* Provide executives with a consolidated view of overall business health.
